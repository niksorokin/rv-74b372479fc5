How it’s deployed

Our servers or yours.

01 · Silow-hosted
Fastest start
  • Install the recorder and go. No infrastructure work on your side.
  • Masking runs on our servers. Raw captures are deleted within 48 hours; only derived patterns are kept.
  • Silow is your data processor under a DPA. Model inference with zero data retention, no training on your data.
From your IT: roll out the recorder to the trial team’s computers
02 · On your servers
Everything inside your perimeter
  • One Docker Compose package, an install README, and an acceptance script that checks every stage.
  • SSO / SAML and audit logs. Screenshots and video never reach Silow.
  • Models on your GPUs, your cloud account, or a zero-data-retention provider you choose.
From your IT: a server for the package, SSO details, a model choice
Workflows with medical records run entirely on your servers, supporting your HIPAA requirements.
